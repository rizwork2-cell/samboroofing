For Your Reference

Image Treatment
Direction

Apply one of these three treatments consistently to all images on your Squarespace site. This is what keeps the brand looking cohesive even when you're uploading real job photos over time.

① Black & White — Primary

Use for all hero images and gallery photos. Keeps the page feeling premium and consistent regardless of lighting conditions in your job photos.

grayscale(100%) contrast(1.1) brightness(0.85)
② Muted Colour — Secondary

For before/after comparisons where colour matters. Desaturated slightly so it still feels on-brand — not raw phone camera colours.

contrast(1.05) saturate(0.85) brightness(0.88)
③ Full Colour — Avoid

Unedited phone photos on a premium site look inconsistent. If you must use full colour, crop tightly and ensure good lighting before uploading.

Use sparingly — team photos only
